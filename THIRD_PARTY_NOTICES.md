# Vocabulary source

The word list and source definitions are attributed to SparkNotes,
*The 1000 Most Common SAT Words*:
https://img.sparknotes.com/content/testprep/pdf/sat.vocab.pdf

The available transcription supplies 990 distinct headwords. Some definitions
have been shortened or corrected for the quiz; source example sentences are
not included. No affiliation or endorsement is claimed.
